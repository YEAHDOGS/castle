The plan

Nine modules. One keep.

ModemGatehouseArmouryVault

01

Gatehouse

The router, rebuilt.

Every packet in your home passes through your keep. Edge hardware, packet management, your front door.

02

Data Vault

Your cloud, at home.

Files, photos, and backups on your own LAN. Self-hosted sync, no monthly fee.

03

LAN cache

Download once.

A game or update pulled once serves the whole house from the keep, not the internet.

04

Drawbridge

An encrypted road home.

Reach your keep from anywhere. Tailscale first, WireGuard as the break-glass backup.

05

Pi-hole DNS

Ads die at the gate.

Trackers, ad networks, and malicious domains dropped before they reach a screen.

06

Armoury

The perimeter.

A firewall at the edge. Access signed on an immutable ledger.

07

Arcade

Every game you own, on a shelf.

A retro library with a digital-bookshelf feel. Saves versioned like git. Browse the shelf.

08

Identity

Profiles with boundaries.

Admins run the keep. Everyone else gets their own rooms, nothing more.

09

LoRa mesh

Reachable off the grid.

Radio nodes keep the castle in touch when the internet is gone.

Questions

Asked, answered.

What is Castle?

A router replacement with your cloud built in. One box behind your modem handles routing, storage, VPN, ad blocking, and a retro arcade for your whole home network.

When does it ship?

Castle is a work in progress. More information is estimated by 2027.

What will it cost?

Target price: $299, with no subscription for the core.

Does it replace my current router?

It sits behind your modem or ONT and takes over routing, firewall, DNS, and storage for your LAN.

Is there an app?

One Castle app for iOS, Android, and web manages the box, your files, and the people on your network.
