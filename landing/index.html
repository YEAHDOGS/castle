Automatic backups

Never lose a contact again.

A dropped phone or a locked account can take years of texts and voicemails with it. Castle backs it all up every night and keeps every version.

  • Texts and picture messages. SMS, MMS, Discord, Messenger.
  • Calls. The full log, and recordings if you make them.
  • Email. Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, Thunderbird.
  • Contacts. Duplicates merged, matched to every message.
  • Computers. Documents and photos, as they change.

Imports work in the demo today. Nightly automatic backup and restoring to a new phone: Building

Your own email server

Outlook went dark. Castle wouldn't have.

On November 12, Outlook, Exchange, Teams and SharePoint went down worldwide, with mail delayed up to 48 hours. Castle runs its own mail server at home, so when a giant has a bad day, your mail doesn't go through the giant.

  • Your own address on your own domain, for the whole family.
  • Bring your old mail. Years of Gmail or Outlook, searchable.
  • Nobody reading it. No ads built from your inbox.

Straight answers

Is Castle ready?

Not yet. The app runs and the demo shows every screen. The box, the mail server, the router and everything marked Building are being made now. The Kickstarter finishes them.

Does it replace Google Drive, iCloud or Dropbox?

That's the heart of it: your files and photos on your own drives, reachable from every device, with no monthly plan.

Do I still need Gmail or Outlook?

Not once Castle's mail server is done. Your family's mail is sent and received by your own box, on your own domain, and your old mail comes with you.

What if my internet goes down?

Files, photos, backups and media keep working inside the house. Mail sent to you waits: the sender's server keeps retrying, normally for days, and it arrives once you're back.

Can my family share one box?

Yes. Everyone gets a private profile with their own files, messages and saves.

Is it open source?

Yes. The code is on GitHub.
