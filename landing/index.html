Presence · Wi‑Fi radar

Know which rooms have someone in them. No cameras.

Small radar nodes on the walls tell Castle when something moves in a room or through a doorway. The app draws each one as a motion‑tracker sweep and keeps a list of rooms, occupied or empty, with when each was entered and left.

In development · the first nodes are being built

  • Tracks up to three people per node by position and speed, about 6 m out and 60° to each side.
  • Senses, never watches. A 24 GHz radar sees movement in the dark and through clutter. No camera, no microphone, no picture of anyone.
  • Reports over your Wi‑Fi to your own box. Nothing leaves the house.
  • Every node holds its own key. It can report what it sees and nothing else. Lose one, replace its key, and the old one stops working at once.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is Castle ready?

Not yet. The app runs and the demo shows every screen. The box itself, the radar nodes and the features marked "in development" are being built now. The Kickstarter is how we finish them.

Do I need a cloud account?

No. Castle lives on your network and keeps your data on your hardware. Tailscale is optional, for reaching the box away from home.

How does the radar work without cameras?

Each node is an ESP32 with a 24 GHz mmWave radar sensor. It measures where things move, not what they look like, and reports over your Wi‑Fi to your box with its own key.

Can my family share one box?

Yes. Everyone gets a profile with their own files, messages and saves. Roles keep them private from each other unless someone chooses to share.

Is it open source?

Yes. The code is on GitHub.
