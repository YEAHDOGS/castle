Automatic backups

Never lose a contact again.

A dropped phone, a dead laptop, an account you got locked out of: that's how families lose years of texts, the last voicemail from someone they loved, and the number of the plumber who actually showed up. Castle backs it all up on its own, every night, and keeps every version.

  • Every text and picture message. SMS, MMS, group chats, plus Discord and Facebook Messenger, kept per person and searchable.

Your own email server

On November 12, Outlook went dark. A Castle would have kept your mail coming.

On Thursday, November 12, Microsoft's email went down around the world. Outlook, Exchange Online, Teams and SharePoint stopped answering for businesses, schools, hospitals and governments, Downdetector took more than 130,000 reports about Outlook alone, and Microsoft had to give mail up to 48 hours to get through. It wasn't a hack. Microsoft said its servers were overloaded, and millions of people found out that their inbox was never really theirs.

Castle runs its own mail server, on the box in your house. Your address, your domain, your inbox, on your hardware. When a giant has a bad day, your mail doesn't go through the giant, so it keeps arriving. And because the same box keeps your texts, calls and chats, every way your family talks lives in one place you own.

  • Your own address on your own domain, for everyone in the family.
  • Bring your old mail with you. Years of Gmail or Outlook, moved onto the box and searchable.
  • Nobody reading over your shoulder. No ads built from your inbox, and no company deciding when you can open it.

Modem and router

The router for the 21st century. Built for a family.

The box your internet comes through decides what every phone, TV and laptop in the house can do, and most of them are a blinking plastic brick with a settings page from 2009. Castle is a modular, expandable modem and router that anyone in the family can understand, with everything a power user would spend a weekend setting up already built in.

  • Modem, router and Wi‑Fi in one. Your internet comes into Castle and goes out to the whole house from there.
  • Ads and trackers blocked for every device on the network, even the TV.
  • Every device, by name and by person. See what's online and how much it's using. Bedtimes and kid-safe browsing per profile.
  • A firewall that answers to you, explained in plain words instead of port numbers.
  • A private way home over Tailscale or WireGuard, set up for you.
  • Modular. Add drives, more compute or new radios as the house grows, to the box you already own.

Today Castle shows its own connection: live traffic, speed, your provider and what is listening. Running the whole network as your modem and router is In development, on Castle OS.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is Castle ready?

Not yet. The app runs and the demo shows every screen. The box itself, the mail server, the router and the features marked "in development" are being built now. The Kickstarter is how we finish them.

Does it replace Google Drive, iCloud or Dropbox?

That's the heart of it. Castle is cloud storage that lives in your house: your files and photos on your own drives, reachable from every device you own, at home or away, with no monthly storage plan.

Do I still need Gmail or Outlook?

Not once Castle's mail server is done. It runs on the box itself, so your family's email is sent and received by your own box, on your own domain. Bring your old mail with you and it stays searchable on the box.

What happens to my email if my internet goes down?

Everything inside the house keeps working: files, photos, backups and media. Mail sent to you while you're offline isn't lost. The sender's mail server keeps trying, normally for days, and it arrives once your connection is back.

Do I need to set up Plex or Jellyfin?

No. Theater is built in. Put your films, shows and music on Castle and they play on your TVs and devices with nothing to install on the box and nothing to configure.

Do I need a cloud account?

No. Castle lives on your network and keeps your data on your hardware. Tailscale is optional, for reaching the box away from home.

Can my family share one box?

Yes. Everyone gets a profile with their own files, messages and saves. Roles keep them private from each other unless someone chooses to share.

Is it open source?

Yes. The code is on GitHub.
