Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · A simple baseline policy, to be expanded as Castle grows.

The short version

This landing page collects one thing, and only if you give it: the email address you put on the waitlist. No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no ads.

The waitlist

When you join the waitlist, your email address is sent to the DOGS API (api.dogs.red, a Cloudflare Worker) and stored in a Cloudflare D1 database with the time you joined. It is used for one thing: telling you when the Castle Kickstarter opens. It is not sold or shared. To be taken off the list, open an issue on the GitHub repository below.

Submitting the form loads Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that a person sent it. It shows no puzzle, and it is not loaded unless you submit the form. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Fonts and hosting

This site is served by Cloudflare Pages, and loads the Poppins and Lora typefaces from Google Fonts. Like most infrastructure providers, they may log basic technical request data (such as IP addresses) to operate their services — see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

When Castle launches for real, this policy will be replaced with a complete one before anything more is collected.
